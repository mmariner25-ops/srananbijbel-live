# Commentary downloads

The Download menu now offers PDF and EPUB editions of the commentary with Scripture quotations, or commentary and references only. The four files are in public/downloads/.

Both EPUBs include the book index, chapter-number buttons, previous/next chapter links and the reading app contents navigation. The PDFs include a clickable book index, chapter-number grids, book/chapter outline bookmarks and footer links back to contents and chapter selection.

Source wording is retained. The Scripture quotations follow the commentary documents, including their original numbering. No new permissions or license have been assigned. Both editions include commentary for 64 books (37 OT and 27 NT); 1 and 2 Chronicles contain links to Kings instead of separate commentary.

With Bible text: 3,211 PDF pages. Without Bible text: 1,837 PDF pages. The latter preserves Scripture quotations that occur inside the author's commentary, but omits the separately interwoven Bible text.

Keep the study-commentary branch selected, copy this project into the repository and replace matching files, then commit, push and review the branch preview. Merge to main only when ready to update the live website.
